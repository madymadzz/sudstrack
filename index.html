About

Built for the shops around the corner

SudsTrack is made for laundry shops near Cubao and the Technological Institute of the Philippines. It handles pickup and delivery scheduling, order status, and payment, so customers stop calling the shop just to ask if their laundry is ready. One link, and it's all there.
