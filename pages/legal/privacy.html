Data Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026  |  SudsTrack Laundry Management System — Cubao / TIP QC

Republic Act 10173 — Data Privacy Act of 2012
SudsTrack is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations, as overseen by the National Privacy Commission (NPC) of the Philippines.

1. Data Controller

SudsTrack ("we," "us," or "our"), operating at Cubao / TIP QC, Quezon City, Philippines, is the personal information controller for all data collected through this platform.

2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data when you register and use SudsTrack:

  • Identity data: Full name, profile picture (optional)
  • Contact data: Email address, mobile number, pickup/delivery address
  • Transaction data: Order history, laundry packages selected, payment method, payment proof (screenshots)
  • Technical data: Login timestamps, IP address (for security logging), browser cookies (JWT authentication)
  • Consent data: Date and time of acceptance of Terms and Privacy Policy

3. Purpose and Legal Basis of Processing

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Contract performance: To process laundry bookings and deliver orders to your address
  • Communications: To send SMS and email notifications about your order status
  • Security: To detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activity
  • Legal compliance: To comply with applicable Philippine laws and regulations
  • Service improvement: To analyze aggregate usage patterns and improve our service

4. How We Share Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your data with:

  • Riders/Delivery staff: Name and contact number for pickup and delivery coordination
  • Service providers: SMS gateway (Semaphore), email service (Gmail SMTP), map services (Google Maps), payment verification
  • Authorities: If required by law, court order, or to protect our rights

All third-party providers are contractually required to maintain the confidentiality and security of your data.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Account data is retained for a minimum of 2 years from the last transaction. You may request deletion of your account and associated data subject to legal retention requirements.

6. Your Rights Under RA 10173

As a data subject, you have the following rights:

  • Right to be informed — to know how your data is collected and used
  • Right to access — to request a copy of your personal data we hold
  • Right to rectification — to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure — to request deletion of your data under certain conditions
  • Right to restrict processing — to limit how we use your data
  • Right to data portability — to obtain your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object — to oppose certain types of processing
  • Right to lodge a complaint — with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us through our Contact page. We will respond within 15 business days.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Password hashing using bcrypt (12 rounds)
  • HttpOnly, Secure cookies for authentication tokens
  • Encrypted database connections (SSL/TLS via Supabase)
  • Account lockout after repeated failed login attempts
  • Role-based access control for staff and administrators

8. Cookies

SudsTrack uses a single HttpOnly authentication cookie (sudstrack_token) to maintain your login session. This cookie is not accessible to JavaScript and expires after 7 days. No third-party advertising or tracking cookies are used.

9. Children's Privacy

SudsTrack is not directed at individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors without parental consent. If you believe a minor has registered without consent, please contact us immediately.

10. Amendments to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of material changes via email. Your continued use of SudsTrack after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact the Privacy Officer

For privacy concerns, data requests, or complaints, please reach us via the Contact page. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.
